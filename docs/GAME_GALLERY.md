**Core mechanics:**
  - Lantern oil is the only resource — every step in unlit water drains a drop, and oil pools are scattered as both reward and risk-pressure
  - Three foes resolved by wit, not damage: Hollowmen (mirror your last move — break by standing still), Brinewraiths (flee from reflected lantern-light off water — angle pools to redirect them), and Choirbones (sing a 3-note pattern you must hum back by stepping on chime-tiles)
  - Tide phases shift mid-run: low tide opens floor passages, high tide opens ceiling vents and submerges shortcuts — read the watermarks on the walls to predict the turn

  - Wicks are your meta-progression — recovered relics become new lantern wicks (longer burn, colored light that reveals hidden glyphs, a wick that hums to soothe Choirbones)
  - Runs end not on death but on lantern-out: you wake on the chapel steps with whatever relics you carried above the waterline

> Wickwater is a tonally cohesive, mechanically novel design with strong atmosphere and a tight 15-minute loop. However, the meta-progression chain has critical gaps: the wickwright crafting recipes that map relics → wicks are entirely implicit, the humming wick has no source relic defined, and the Lower Ossuary requires three different wicks simultaneously despite wicks appearing mutually exclusive. Quest 'quest_unfinished_compline' is currently uncompletable as wired. Several puzzle mechanics (sconce ordering, Brinewraith reflection) are under-specified. Marked not playable pending recipe table, wick-swap mechanism, and the missing upper→lower dungeon connection. Once those four issues are addressed, this is a 9/10 design.

### Issues found (12)

1. **[major]** Quest 'quest_unfinished_compline' requires equipping item_wick_colored (Wick of Saint-Glass), but no quest, drop, or wickwright recipe in the data explicitly produces this wick. Relics produced are bone (rare wick), glass (rare wick — likely intended source), and silver (epic wick), but the mapping from item_relic_glass → item_wick_colored is implicit and never declared in any reward chain or crafting recipe. Players may collect glass relics and never know they yield the colored wick required to enter the Lower Ossuary.
2. **[major]** item_wick_humming (Wick of the Unfinished Compline) is required to pass room_low_silent_choir but has no defined source relic. No relic in items[] is tagged as 'choir-string' or otherwise marked as producing the humming wick. Its lore mentions 'choir-string' but no such item exists in the data — the wick is unobtainable as defined.
3. **[major]** Quest 'quest_unfinished_compline' has prerequisite 'quest_three_dim_shrines' but requires the Wick of Saint-Glass (colored) AND a humming wick AND a long-burn wick. These wicks come from relics recovered via 'quest_mosaic_and_seven_saints' and later runs, but quest_mosaic_and_seven_saints is NOT a prerequisite of quest_unfinished_compline. Players could accept the quest with no path to complete it.
4. **[major]** obj_light_sconces in quest_mosaic_and_seven_saints has target 'entity_sconce_1' with count 3 — but the puzzle requires lighting THREE SPECIFIC sconces out of seven in a specific order determined by the floor mosaic. Targeting only entity_sconce_1 with count 3 either means 'light sconce 1 three times' (nonsensical) or is shorthand the engine can't disambiguate. The order requirement is also not encoded.
5. **[major]** Oil drain economy is under-specified and likely unbalanced. The lantern starts with an unspecified reserve, drains 1 drop per step in unlit water, and item_oil_flask restores 60 drops. With no stated starting oil and no per-room step counts, it's impossible to verify a 15-minute run is achievable. The Long Wade explicitly 'longer than any single oil reserve can cross' unless using long-burn wick — meaning a baseline player attempting the Lower Ossuary without that specific wick equipped will softlock or be forced to retreat, but the failure_conditions don't include 'lantern_out_in_lower_wade' as a graceful state.
6. **[minor]** npc_keepers_journal_voice has location_id 'room_chapel_threshold' but its inventory contains 'item_keepers_journal', which is also listed as an entity ('entity_keeper_journal') in the same room. Whether the journal is an NPC, an item, or an entity is ambiguous — three references to the same object with no clear authoritative form.
7. **[minor]** Quest reward for quest_listen_to_the_wall grants TWO item_tidemark_chalk, but the quest's own obj_mark_phase consumes one chalk (objective_type: 'craft' on item_tidemark_chalk). Net: player enters with 1 chalk, uses 1, receives 2 = +1 net. Acceptable, but the 'craft' objective_type is wrong for a consumable use — should be 'use' or 'interact'.
8. **[minor]** dungeon_sunken_ossuary has no connection from room_oss_landing to room_oss_great_doors documented as 'requires lantern lit' at the connection level — only the room property 'requires_lantern_lit: true' on the exit room. If a player reaches the doors with the lantern out, the run is already over (lantern-out triggers wake-on-steps). The 'requires_lantern_lit' flag is therefore redundant and may confuse engine logic that checks it.
9. **[minor]** npc_brother_cael has inventory containing item_tidemark_chalk but the quest_listen_to_the_wall grants chalk as reward from completing the quest with Cael. Whether the chalk in Cael's inventory is given on first meeting, after the chalk mark, or is purely flavor is unclear. Could lead to duplicate or missing chalk grants.
10. **[minor]** Brinewraith resolution ('angle reflected lantern light off water pools') is mechanically vague. The data declares 'entity_pool_north' and 'entity_pool_west' in the Reflecting Nave but doesn't specify how the lantern's reflection vector is computed, whether the player's facing direction matters, or how 'herd them aside' resolves to a clear win-state. The mechanic is the most novel of the three foes and the most under-specified.
11. **[minor]** Wicks are mutually exclusive (only one equipped at the lantern at a time, implied by 'category: accessory, max_stack: 1'). The Lower Ossuary requires colored wick to enter (Glyph Landing), humming wick to pass Silent Choir, AND long-burn wick to cross Long Wade. These cannot all be equipped simultaneously. The dungeon is unbeatable as designed unless wicks can be swapped mid-run, which is undocumented.
12. **[minor]** quest_unfinished_compline.branches has 'let_her_finish' → 'ending_compline_completed' and 'keep_the_relic' → 'ending_relic_carried' but neither ending is defined elsewhere in the data, nor is the branch decision point in the objective list. Players have no in-quest moment shown where they choose.
